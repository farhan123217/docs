GBP Auditor (temporary working name) is a free Chrome Extension for Local SEO professionals who want to audit Google Business Profiles and Google Maps search results without paying for APIs or surrendering data to third parties. It reads only the information already visible on the page, scores it against a 100-point framework, and generates actionable reports that stay on your device.

This site is the single source of truth for the product specification, architecture, and build plan. The name and logo are intentionally swappable through a single branding configuration file, so the product can be rebranded without touching code logic.

## Core principles

These constraints shape every feature and engineering decision in GBP Auditor:

**Zero cost.** The extension never requires a paid Google Maps API, SEO data provider, proxy, or scraping service. Analysis is performed locally in the browser using DOM extraction of publicly visible information. Optional, user-provided API keys may be supported later for advanced features, but the core product must work without them.

**Visible data only.** GBP Auditor reads only what is already rendered on the page for the logged-in or logged-out user. It never bypasses CAPTCHA, login walls, rate limits, anti-bot protections, or private account data. If a field is not visible, it is marked "N/A" or "Not detected".

**Read-only analysis.** The product never auto-modifies a Google Business Profile, simulates unauthorized clicks, or impersonates a user. It audits, compares, reports, and exports. It does not post, edit, claim, or delete.

**Never invent data.** When extraction fails or a page is unsupported, the extension surfaces a clear message. It does not hallucinate scores, ranking causation, or guaranteed outcomes. Recommendations are framed as "potential optimization opportunities."
